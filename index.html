Our Artists

Dejan Krstić

Blackwork & Geometric

Dejan specializes in bold blackwork, geometric patterns, and highly detailed designs. His work combines clean lines with strong contrast, creating tattoos that feel both modern and timeless.

Lena Janeva

Our Studio

Craft over volume.

INK House opened its doors in 2019. Since day one, we've never run promotions, we are walk-ins friendly, and we close on Sundays.

Our artists work with a full consultation process: you bring a reference, we discuss your ideas and placement, then we sketch, refine, and prepare every detail before the needle touches your skin. The tattoo we do together is the tattoo you'll have forever — it should be worth the wait.

We keep the studio small because big studios make compromises. We don't.
